The ablation study evaluated GPT-5 and Gemini 2.5 Pro across six prompt variants and six HeCBench benchmarks, yielding 72 experimental runs in total (2 models × 6 variants × 6 apps). Across all successful runs, GPT-5 demonstrated consistently higher first-attempt success rates, requiring only a single build iteration in 32 of 36 cases compared to Gemini's 24 of 36. Gemini exhausted all three repair iterations on 8 runs (4 failures, 4 eventually repaired), with sobol being its most problematic benchmark (3 outright failures due to correctness violations and a persistent `\_\_builtin\_ctz` host-device mismatch).
