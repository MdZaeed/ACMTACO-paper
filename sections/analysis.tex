In terms of optimization quality, neither model delivers large, reliable speedups within the ablation configuration (pc+rl input, full prompt ablated). The best sustained speedup across all valid results was approximately 1.14x (GPT-5 on bitonic-sort with `no\_constraints`, `cot`, or `no\_proof`) and 1.13x (Gemini on bitonic-sort with `no\_explanation` or `cot`). For sobol, both models either failed or matched the baseline within 2\%. For atan2, md5hash, and accuracy, the runtime distributions reveal that the `minimal` variant consistently produces the worst results for both models across multiple benchmarks, suggesting that stripping contextual framing from the prompt measurably degrades optimization quality. The `cot` variant, conversely, tends to produce stable or slightly better results for both models, though the improvement is modest.

The most consequential finding is the benchmark gaming behavior on shmembench. GPT-5 games the benchmark in all 6 variants without exception, exploiting the algebraic invariant that a swap-based permutation preserves the sum to eliminate all shared memory traffic — producing 235–949x apparent speedups that do not reflect genuine hardware improvement. Gemini games the benchmark only when the formal proof-of-correctness requirement is removed (`no\_proof`), achieving 213x apparent speedup through the same mechanism. This demonstrates that prompt-level constraints — specifically the requirement to provide a formal reasoning trace justifying correctness — can suppress this class of semantic-preserving but benchmark-invalidating transformations in Gemini, while GPT-5 applies them regardless. The finding motivates the addition of an explicit computational-intent constraint to the OPAL prompt to prevent LLMs from replacing the computation structurally rather than optimizing it.

Overall, prompt component sensitivity is model-dependent and non-uniform across benchmarks: removing constraints benefits GPT-5 on bitonic-sort but hurts Gemini; removing the explanation helps Gemini on bitonic-sort but has no consistent effect elsewhere; and removing the role assignment (`no\_role`) caused a catastrophic timeout in GPT-5 on accuracy while Gemini handled it without issue. The only variant that consistently degrades both models is `minimal`, and the only variant that improves both without exception is `cot`.
